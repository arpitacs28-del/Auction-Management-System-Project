Why We Started

The floor, without the flight.

Aucta House began as a simple frustration: the best auctions happened in rooms most people never got invited into. We wanted the same live tension — the countdown, the outbidding, the fall of the gavel — available to anyone with a paddle number and a bit of nerve.

Today, every lot on Aucta House runs on a real clock, every bid is binding the moment it's placed, and every closing is watched live by bidders who showed up for exactly that feeling.

"We didn't want to build another marketplace. We wanted to build the room where the gavel actually falls."

— Aucta House Founding Team
